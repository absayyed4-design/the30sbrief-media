The day Blackbeard fought his last battle 🏴‍☠️ 📜

On November 22, 1718, the pirate Blackbeard was killed in a battle near Ocracoke Island, North Carolina. Virginia Governor Alexander Spotswood had sent two sloops after him under Lieutenant Robert Maynard of the Royal Navy, and the fight ended hand to hand on deck. Blackbeard was known for tucking slow-burning fuses under his hat to frighten his enemies, and earlier that year he had blockaded Charleston harbor. Centuries later, divers found his flagship, Queen Anne's Revenge, off the North Carolina coast.
