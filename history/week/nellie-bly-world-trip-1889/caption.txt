Around the world in 72 days, with one small bag 🌍 📜

On November 14, 1889, New York World reporter Nellie Bly set out to beat the fictional 80-day trip in Jules Verne's novel. Traveling with a single small bag, she met Verne himself in Amiens, France, along the way. She arrived back on January 25, 1890, after about 72 days, ahead of rival reporter Elizabeth Bisland, who raced the other way.
