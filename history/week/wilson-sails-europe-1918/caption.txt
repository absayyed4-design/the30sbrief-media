A president crosses the Atlantic for the first time 🚢 📜

On December 4, 1918, President Woodrow Wilson sailed for France aboard the George Washington, the first sitting U.S. president to travel to Europe. After nine days at sea he reached Brest and went on to the Paris Peace Conference after World War I, where he pushed for a League of Nations. Back home, the Senate rejected the Treaty of Versailles.
