Chicago almost ended up outside Illinois 🗺️ 📜

On December 3, 1818, Illinois became the 21st state. Before statehood, its delegate in Congress, Nathaniel Pope, won a change that moved the northern border about 51 miles north, giving Illinois a stretch of Lake Michigan shoreline and the future site of Chicago. The state capital moved to Springfield in 1837.

Did you know Chicago almost wasn't in Illinois?
