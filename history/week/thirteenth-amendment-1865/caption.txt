The day freedom was written into the Constitution 🕊️ 📜

On December 6, 1865, Georgia became the 27th state to ratify the 13th Amendment, reaching the three-fourths of states needed to make it law and abolish slavery in the United States. The House of Representatives had passed the amendment on January 31, 1865. President Abraham Lincoln, who signed the resolution before sending it to the states, was killed that April and did not live to see it ratified.

Did you know the 13th Amendment took most of 1865 to become law?
