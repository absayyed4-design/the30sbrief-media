444 days that held a nation's attention 🕯️ 📜

On November 4, 1979, Iranian students stormed the United States Embassy in Tehran and took dozens of Americans captive. Fifty-two of them were held for 444 days. They were freed on January 20, 1981, minutes after Ronald Reagan was sworn in as president, and all 52 came home.
