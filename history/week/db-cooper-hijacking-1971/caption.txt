The hijacker who jumped into the night and vanished 🪂 📜

On November 24, 1971, the day before Thanksgiving, a man who bought his ticket as "Dan Cooper" hijacked Northwest Orient Flight 305 from Portland to Seattle. He demanded $200,000 and four parachutes, let the 36 passengers go in Seattle, then jumped from the rear stairs of the Boeing 727 over southwest Washington. In 1980, about $5,800 of the ransom was found on a Columbia River bank. The FBI stopped actively investigating in 2016, and the man known as D. B. Cooper was never identified.
