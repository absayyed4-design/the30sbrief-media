The day America's dry spell ended 🍻 📜

On December 5, 1933, Utah became the 36th state to ratify the 21st Amendment, the final vote needed to repeal the 18th Amendment and end national Prohibition. The ban on alcohol sales had been in force since January 1920. Control then passed to the states, and some stayed dry for years. Mississippi was the last, ending its statewide ban in 1966.

Did you know Prohibition lasted almost 14 years?
