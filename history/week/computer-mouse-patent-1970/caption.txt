The first computer mouse was made of wood 🖱️ 📜

On November 17, 1970, Douglas Engelbart received U.S. Patent 3,541,541 for an "X-Y position indicator for a display system," better known as the computer mouse. The prototype was a wooden shell with two metal wheels, built with engineer Bill English, and his team named it the mouse because of its tail. His employer SRI later licensed it to Apple for about $40,000, and Engelbart never received royalties.
