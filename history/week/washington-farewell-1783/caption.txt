The day Washington said goodbye 🇺🇸 📜

On December 4, 1783, George Washington gathered his officers in the Long Room of Fraunces Tavern in New York City and asked each one to come forward and take his hand. Witnesses said he was in tears as he embraced them. On December 23 he resigned his command in Annapolis and went home to Mount Vernon. Fraunces Tavern is a museum today.

Could you walk away from that much power?
