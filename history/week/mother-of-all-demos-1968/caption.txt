The 1968 demo that showed the future of computers 🖱️ 📜

On December 9, 1968, engineer Douglas Engelbart of the Stanford Research Institute gave a 90-minute live demonstration in San Francisco to about 1,000 computer professionals. Using a wooden mouse with two wheels, he showed windows, hypertext links, word processing, shared editing and live video calls with his team back at SRI in Menlo Park. It became known as "The Mother of All Demos," and ideas from it now shape the computers and phones we use every day.
