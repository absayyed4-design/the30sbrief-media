The year America had two Thanksgivings 🦃 📜

On November 23, 1939, many Americans sat down to Thanksgiving dinner a week early. President Franklin D. Roosevelt had moved the holiday from the last Thursday of November (November 30 that year) to the one before it, to give stores a longer Christmas shopping season after hard economic years. Many states refused and kept November 30, Texas observed both dates, and critics dubbed it "Franksgiving." On December 26, 1941, a new federal law fixed Thanksgiving on the fourth Thursday of November.
