She was six years old, and she changed history 🎒 📜

On November 14, 1960, six-year-old Ruby Bridges walked into William Frantz Elementary School in New Orleans, escorted by four federal marshals, past an angry crowd. Almost every white family pulled their children out. For the rest of the school year, teacher Barbara Henry taught Ruby alone in her classroom. Norman Rockwell later painted her famous walk in "The Problem We All Live With."

Could you have been that brave at six?
