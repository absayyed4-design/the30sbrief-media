The day America said goodbye to JFK 🕯️ 📜

On November 25, 1963, three days after his assassination in Dallas, President John F. Kennedy was laid to rest at Arlington National Cemetery. A horse-drawn caisson carried his casket, and leaders from 92 nations came to mourn. Outside St. Matthew's Cathedral, his son John Jr., who turned three that day, saluted his father's casket. At the grave, Jacqueline Kennedy lit an eternal flame that still burns today.
