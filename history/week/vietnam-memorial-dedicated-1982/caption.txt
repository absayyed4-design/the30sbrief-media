The Wall that honors more than 58,000 names 🇺🇸 📜

On November 13, 1982, the Vietnam Veterans Memorial was dedicated in Washington, D.C. Its design, by Yale student Maya Lin, was chosen from more than 1,400 entries. The black granite Wall now carries more than 58,000 names of Americans killed or missing in the war. Some critics first called the plain design too somber, but that criticism faded over time.
