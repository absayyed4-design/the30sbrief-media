The radio show that made country music famous 🎻 📜

On November 28, 1925, Nashville station WSM aired the first broadcast of what announcer George D. Hay called the WSM Barn Dance. Its opening act was 77-year-old fiddler Uncle Jimmy Thompson. In December 1927, Hay renamed the show the Grand Ole Opry. It played at the Ryman Auditorium until 1974, and a century later it is still on the air every week.
