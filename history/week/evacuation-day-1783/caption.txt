The day the British finally left New York 🇺🇸 📜

On November 25, 1783, the last British troops sailed out of New York City, their final stronghold in the former Thirteen Colonies after seven years of occupation. George Washington then rode in with the Continental Army as crowds cheered. Legend says the departing British left their flag on a greased pole, and veteran John Van Arsdale climbed it to raise the Stars and Stripes. New Yorkers celebrated "Evacuation Day" for more than a century.
