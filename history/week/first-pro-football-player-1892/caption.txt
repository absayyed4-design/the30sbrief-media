Pro football started with $500 🏈 📜

On November 12, 1892, the Allegheny Athletic Association paid three-time Yale All-America guard William "Pudge" Heffelfinger $500 to play one game against the rival Pittsburgh Athletic Club. He scored the only touchdown, and he is the first known paid football player. An old club ledger recording the payment still survives, and the Pro Football Hall of Fame calls it pro football's birth certificate.
