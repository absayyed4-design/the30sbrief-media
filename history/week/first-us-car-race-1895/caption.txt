America's first car race was run in the snow 🚗 📜

On November 28, 1895, Thanksgiving Day, the Chicago Times-Herald held the first automobile race in the United States. After a snowstorm, only six cars made it to the start in Chicago for a 54-mile round trip to Evanston and back. Just two finished. Frank Duryea won in his American-built motor wagon after about ten hours on snowy roads, taking home the top prize.
