The night America first heard an election on the radio 📻 📜

On November 2, 1920, Westinghouse station KDKA in Pittsburgh read the Harding vs Cox presidential election returns live on the air, as results were phoned in from a newspaper office. The broadcast came from a small shed on a factory roof, and it is widely called the first commercial radio broadcast. Warren G. Harding won in a landslide, and by 1922 about 500 radio stations were on the air across the country.
