Remembering Pearl Harbor 🇺🇸 📜

On December 7, 1941, Japanese aircraft attacked the U.S. Navy fleet at Pearl Harbor, Hawaii, just before 8 a.m. on a Sunday morning. More than 2,400 Americans were killed and about 1,200 were wounded. On the battleship USS Arizona alone, 1,177 crew members died. The next day, Congress declared war on Japan, and the United States entered World War II. Today, the USS Arizona Memorial rests above the sunken ship.
