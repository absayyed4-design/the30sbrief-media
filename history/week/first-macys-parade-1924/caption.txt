The first Macy's parade had real elephants 🐘 📜

On November 27, 1924, Macy's held its very first holiday parade in New York City. Store employees marched about six miles from Harlem down to the flagship store at Herald Square, and more than 250,000 people lined the route. There were no giant balloons yet. Instead, elephants, camels, bears and monkeys were borrowed from the Central Park Zoo, and Santa Claus arrived at the end. Balloons replaced the animals in 1927, starting with Felix the Cat.
