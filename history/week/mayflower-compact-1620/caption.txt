41 men, one promise, and the roots of self-government ⚓ 📜

On November 11, 1620 (by the calendar used at the time), 41 men aboard the Mayflower signed the Mayflower Compact while anchored off Cape Cod. The ship had landed far north of the Virginia territory it was headed for, so the passengers agreed to make and obey their own laws together. It became the first governing document of Plymouth Colony and an early step toward self-government in America.
