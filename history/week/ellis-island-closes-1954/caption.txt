America's most famous front door closes 🗽 📜

On November 12, 1954, the Ellis Island immigration station in New York Harbor shut down after 62 years. Since 1892, about 12 million immigrants had entered the United States through its halls. The last person to leave was Arne Peterssen, a Norwegian sailor who had overstayed his shore leave and was sent home to Norway.
