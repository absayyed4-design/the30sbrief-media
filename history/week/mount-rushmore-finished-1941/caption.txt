The day they put down the tools on Mount Rushmore 🗿 📜

On October 31, 1941, work on Mount Rushmore came to an end after 14 years of blasting and drilling in South Dakota's Black Hills. About 400 people worked on the mountain, and not one of them died on the job. Sculptor Gutzon Borglum had died that March, so his son Lincoln Borglum led the final months until the money ran out and the carving was declared finished.
