The day a meteorite hit a woman on her couch ☄️ 📜

On November 30, 1954, a meteorite crashed through the roof of a house in Sylacauga, Alabama, bounced off a console radio and struck Ann Hodges as she napped on her couch. The space rock weighed about 8.5 pounds and left her with a large bruise on her hip. She became the first person known to be injured by a meteorite, and the rock now sits in the Alabama Museum of Natural History.
