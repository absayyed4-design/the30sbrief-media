Thanksgiving on November 26, just like in 1789 🦃 📜

On November 26, 1789, Americans observed the first Thanksgiving under the new Constitution. At the request of Congress, President George Washington had proclaimed that Thursday a day of public thanksgiving and prayer. It did not become a yearly event right away. Exactly 74 years later, Abraham Lincoln's first national Thanksgiving also fell on November 26, in 1863, and his proclamation made the last Thursday of November an annual holiday. This year, Thanksgiving falls on Thursday, November 26 once again.
