Five Navy planes took off from Florida and never came back ✈️ 📜

On December 5, 1945, Flight 19, five U.S. Navy Avenger torpedo bombers with 14 airmen, left Naval Air Station Fort Lauderdale on a training flight. Their leader, Lt. Charles C. Taylor, lost his bearings when his compasses failed, and the planes were lost at sea. A PBM Mariner search plane with 13 men aboard was also lost that night. No wreckage was ever confirmed, and the mystery helped build the legend of the Bermuda Triangle.
