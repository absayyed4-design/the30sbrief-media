The whale that sank a ship and inspired Moby-Dick 🐋 📜

On November 20, 1820, a sperm whale rammed the Nantucket whaleship Essex twice in the middle of the Pacific Ocean, and the ship went down. Twenty men escaped in three small whaleboats, far from any land. After months adrift, only 8 of the 21 crewmen survived. First mate Owen Chase published their story in 1821, and it later helped inspire Herman Melville's Moby-Dick (1851).
