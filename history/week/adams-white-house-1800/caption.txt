The first family to call the White House home 🏛️ 📜

On November 1, 1800, John Adams became the first president to live in the White House, then called the President's House and still unfinished eight years after its cornerstone was laid in 1792. On November 2, Adams wrote to his wife Abigail hoping that only honest and wise men would ever rule under its roof, a blessing later carved into the State Dining Room mantel. When Abigail arrived, she dried the family laundry in the empty East Room. The Adamses lived there only a few months before Thomas Jefferson moved in.
