Casablanca opened on this day in 1942 🎬 📜

On November 26, 1942, Casablanca premiered at the Hollywood Theater in New York City. Warner Bros. rushed it out just weeks after Allied forces landed in North Africa. Humphrey Bogart and Ingrid Bergman starred as Rick and Ilsa, former lovers torn apart by the war. The film went on to win three Oscars, including Best Picture.
