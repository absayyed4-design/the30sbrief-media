The day America lost President Kennedy 🕯️ 📜

On November 22, 1963, President John F. Kennedy and First Lady Jacqueline Kennedy rode through Dallas, Texas, past cheering crowds. At about 12:30 p.m., shots were fired as the motorcade passed through Dealey Plaza, and the 46-year-old president, the 35th, was pronounced dead at Parkland Memorial Hospital at 1:00 p.m. That afternoon, aboard Air Force One at Love Field, Lyndon B. Johnson took the oath of office from Judge Sarah T. Hughes, with Jacqueline Kennedy at his side.
