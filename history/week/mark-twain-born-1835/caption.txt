The birthday of America's great storyteller 📚 📜

On November 30, 1835, Samuel Clemens was born in Florida, Missouri, and grew up in Hannibal beside the Mississippi River. His pen name, Mark Twain, came from riverboat slang for water two fathoms, or 12 feet, deep. He wrote The Adventures of Tom Sawyer and Adventures of Huckleberry Finn. Halley's Comet passed the sun just before his birth, and he died in 1910, one day after it returned.
