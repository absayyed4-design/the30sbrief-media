At 77, John Glenn went back to space 🚀 📜

On October 29, 1998, former Mercury astronaut John Glenn launched aboard Space Shuttle Discovery on mission STS-95, 36 years after he became the first American to orbit Earth in Friendship 7. At 77, he was the oldest person to fly in space at the time. During the nine-day flight, researchers studied how spaceflight affects an aging body.
