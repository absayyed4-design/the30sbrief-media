The day astronauts photographed the whole Earth 🌍 📜

On December 7, 1972, just hours after launch, the crew of Apollo 17 photographed the fully lit Earth from about 18,000 miles away. The image, later known as the Blue Marble, became one of the most reproduced photos in history. Apollo 17, flown by Gene Cernan, Ronald Evans and Harrison Schmitt, was the last crewed mission to the Moon.
