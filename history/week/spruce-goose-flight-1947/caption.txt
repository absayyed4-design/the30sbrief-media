The giant wooden plane that flew just once ✈️ 📜

On November 2, 1947, Howard Hughes took the controls of the H-4 Hercules, the huge flying boat nicknamed the Spruce Goose. Built mostly of birch because of wartime metal limits, it had a 320-foot wingspan. Over Long Beach Harbor it rose about 70 feet and flew about a mile in under a minute. It never flew again and now sits at the Evergreen Aviation and Space Museum in McMinnville, Oregon.
