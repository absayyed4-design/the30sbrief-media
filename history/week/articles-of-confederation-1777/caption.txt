America had a rulebook before the Constitution 🏛️ 📜

On November 15, 1777, the Continental Congress, which had moved to York, Pennsylvania while British troops held Philadelphia, adopted the Articles of Confederation. All 13 states had to ratify it, and it only took effect in March 1781 after Maryland signed on last. Congress could not even collect taxes under it, and in 1789 the U.S. Constitution took its place.
