The Civil War battle fought in the clouds ⛰️ 📜

On November 24, 1863, Union troops under General Joseph Hooker attacked up the steep slopes of Lookout Mountain near Chattanooga, Tennessee. Heavy fog rolled in during the fighting, making it hard even for General Ulysses S. Grant to follow the battle, and it became known as the "Battle Above the Clouds." The Union won the mountain, and the Confederates pulled back to Missionary Ridge, where Union forces attacked the next day.
