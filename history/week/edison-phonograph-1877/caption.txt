The day Thomas Edison taught a machine to talk back 🎙️ 📜

On November 21, 1877, Thomas Edison announced his phonograph, a machine that could record sound and play it back. A needle cut the sound into tinfoil wrapped around a turning cylinder, and his first test was the nursery rhyme "Mary Had a Little Lamb." Edison received his patent for the phonograph on February 19, 1878.

Could you live without recorded music?
