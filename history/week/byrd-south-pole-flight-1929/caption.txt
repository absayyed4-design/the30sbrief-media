The first flight over the South Pole ✈️ 📜

On November 29, 1929, Richard E. Byrd and his crew became the first to fly over the South Pole. Pilot Bernt Balchen, co-pilot Harold June and photographer Ashley McKinley flew with navigator Byrd from their base, Little America, in a Ford Trimotor named Floyd Bennett. To climb over the high mountain pass, they dumped bags of food. The round trip took almost 19 hours, and Byrd was promoted to rear admiral soon after.
