The most famous wrong headline in American history 🗞️ 📜

On November 3, 1948, the Chicago Daily Tribune's early edition ran the banner "DEWEY DEFEATS TRUMAN." A printers' strike pushed the paper to press early, before most results were in, and the polls had favored Thomas E. Dewey. About 150,000 copies were printed. But Harry S. Truman had won, and at St. Louis Union Station he held up a copy for the cameras.
