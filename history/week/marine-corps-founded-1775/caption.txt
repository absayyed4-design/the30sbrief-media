Happy birthday, Marines 🇺🇸 📜

On November 10, 1775, the Continental Congress in Philadelphia called for two battalions of Marines, the start of what became the United States Marine Corps. Tradition says the first recruits signed up at Tun Tavern, and Samuel Nicholas became the first leader of the Continental Marines. Marines around the world still celebrate November 10 as their birthday.

Do you know a Marine? Wish them a happy birthday.
