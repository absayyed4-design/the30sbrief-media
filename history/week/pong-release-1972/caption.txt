The video game that jammed with quarters 🕹️ 📜

On November 29, 1972, Atari released Pong, a simple table tennis game often called the first commercially successful video game. It began as a practice project that co-founder Nolan Bushnell gave new engineer Allan Alcorn. When the test machine at Andy Capp's Tavern in California stopped working, the problem was a coin box overflowing with quarters. By the end of 1974, Atari had sold more than 8,000 Pong machines.
