The astronauts who visited a robot on the Moon 🌕 📜

On November 19, 1969, Apollo 12 astronauts Pete Conrad and Alan Bean landed in the Ocean of Storms while Richard Gordon orbited above. Five days earlier, lightning had struck their Saturn V twice during launch. They touched down within walking distance of Surveyor 3, a robot probe that landed in 1967, and brought pieces of it home, including its TV camera.
