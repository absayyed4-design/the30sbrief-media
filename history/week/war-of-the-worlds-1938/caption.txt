The night Martians "invaded" New Jersey 👽 📜

On October 30, 1938, 23-year-old Orson Welles and the Mercury Theatre on the Air performed "The War of the Worlds" on CBS radio, told as fake news bulletins about Martians landing at Grovers Mill, New Jersey. Newspapers reported mass hysteria the next day. Historians now say the panic was far smaller than claimed: one ratings survey found only about 2 percent of people were tuned in, and papers losing ad money to radio had reason to play it up. Some listeners were truly frightened, but nationwide panic is mostly a myth.
