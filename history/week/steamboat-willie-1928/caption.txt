The cartoon that made Mickey Mouse a star 🐭 📜

On November 18, 1928, Steamboat Willie premiered at the Colony Theatre in New York, Mickey Mouse's public debut. The 7 minute 47 second short was not the first cartoon with synchronized sound, but it brought the new technology to a huge audience, and Walt Disney did the voices himself. On January 1, 2024, the film entered the public domain in the United States.
