The guns fell silent at 11 a.m. 🕊️ 📜

On November 11, 1918, the armistice ending the fighting of World War One took effect at 11 a.m. American soldiers in France celebrated, and crowds filled the streets back home. The war had cost more than 116,000 American lives. November 11 became Armistice Day, a national holiday in 1938, and was renamed Veterans Day in 1954 to honor all who served.
