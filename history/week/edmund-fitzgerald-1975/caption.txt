Remembering the 29 men of the Edmund Fitzgerald 🕯️ 📜

On November 10, 1975, the freighter SS Edmund Fitzgerald sank in a fierce storm on Lake Superior. When she was launched in 1958, she was the largest ship on the Great Lakes. All 29 crew members were lost, and the wreck was found in about 530 feet of water. A year later, Gordon Lightfoot's song "The Wreck of the Edmund Fitzgerald" made their story known around the world.
