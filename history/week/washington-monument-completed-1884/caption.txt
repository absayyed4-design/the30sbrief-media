It took 36 years to finish the Washington Monument 🏛️ 📜

On December 6, 1884, workers set the capstone and a small aluminum tip on top of the Washington Monument, completing the 555-foot obelisk. Construction had started on July 4, 1848, but stopped from 1854 to 1877 because of money problems and the Civil War. When it was finished, it was the tallest man-made structure in the world, until the Eiffel Tower passed it five years later.
