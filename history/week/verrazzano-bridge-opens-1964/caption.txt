The bridge built around the curve of the Earth 🌉 📜

On November 21, 1964, the Verrazzano-Narrows Bridge opened in New York City, linking Brooklyn and Staten Island. Its main span of 4,260 feet was the longest suspension span in the world when it opened. Designed by engineer Othmar Ammann, its towers are about 1 5/8 inches farther apart at the top than at the base, to allow for the curve of the Earth.
