One seat that changed America 🚌 📜

On December 1, 1955, Rosa Parks, a seamstress riding home from work, refused to give up her bus seat in Montgomery, Alabama, and was arrested. Her arrest sparked the Montgomery bus boycott, which lasted 381 days and was led by the Montgomery Improvement Association under a young pastor, Martin Luther King Jr. The courts struck down segregated seating, and the city's buses were integrated in December 1956.
