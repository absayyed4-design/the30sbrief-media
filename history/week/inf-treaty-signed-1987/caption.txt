The day the Cold War rivals agreed to scrap nuclear missiles ✍️ 📜

On December 8, 1987, President Ronald Reagan and Soviet leader Mikhail Gorbachev signed the Intermediate-Range Nuclear Forces (INF) Treaty at the White House. It banned a whole category of ground-launched nuclear missiles, and by the June 1, 1991 deadline the two countries had destroyed 2,692 of them. It was the first time the superpowers agreed to actually reduce their nuclear arsenals, with on-site inspections to check.
