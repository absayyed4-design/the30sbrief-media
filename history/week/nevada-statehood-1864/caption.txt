How Nevada became the Battle Born State ⭐ 📜

On October 31, 1864, Nevada joined the Union as the 36th state, in the middle of the Civil War. With the presidential election only days away, its state constitution was sent to Washington by telegraph, the longest telegram on record at the time. President Lincoln wanted Nevada's support for his reelection and for the 13th Amendment to end slavery, even though the territory had only about 40,000 people.
