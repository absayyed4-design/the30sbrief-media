The explorer who never climbed his own mountain 🏔️ 📜

On November 15, 1806, Army Lieutenant Zebulon Pike spotted a distant peak in present-day Colorado that looked like a small blue cloud. His party climbed a nearby mountain in waist-deep snow, then turned back, and Pike wrote that no one could reach the summit. Edwin James did it in 1820, and in 1893 the view from the top inspired Katharine Lee Bates to write America the Beautiful.
