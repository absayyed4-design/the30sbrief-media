The day two territories got married 💍 📜

On November 16, 1907, President Theodore Roosevelt signed the proclamation that joined Oklahoma Territory and Indian Territory, and Oklahoma became the 46th state. In Guthrie, a huge crowd watched a symbolic wedding: Charles Jones stood for Mr. Oklahoma Territory and Anna Bennett, of Cherokee descent, for Miss Indian Territory. Charles N. Haskell was then sworn in as the first governor.
