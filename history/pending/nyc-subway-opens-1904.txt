Caption:
The day New York went underground for a nickel 🚇 📜

On October 27, 1904, New York City opened its first subway line. Mayor George McClellan drove the first train out of City Hall, and the line ran 9.1 miles through 28 stations up to 145th Street in Harlem. A ride cost five cents, and more than 100,000 people paid to ride once it opened to the public that evening.
