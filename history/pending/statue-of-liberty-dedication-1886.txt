Caption:
The day Lady Liberty was unveiled to America 🗽 📜

On October 28, 1886, President Grover Cleveland dedicated the Statue of Liberty in New York Harbor, a gift from the people of France. The copper statue stands 151 feet tall. Sculptor Auguste Bartholdi waited inside her crown to drop the French flag from her face and let it fall early, in the middle of a speech. That morning's parade gave New York City its first ticker-tape parade.
